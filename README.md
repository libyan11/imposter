# Imposter

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/imposter/)

https://libyan11.github.io/imposter/

A party word game for 3 to 10 players, in your browser on phone or computer. Everyone gets the same secret word, except one imposter. Say one word each, then vote out the faker. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Two ways to play**
- **Online room:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. Everyone plays on their own phone, typing clues (or saying them out loud if you're together).
- **Pass one phone:** add everyone's names, then pass the phone so each person sees their card. Works offline after the first visit.

**Rules**
- Everyone sees the secret word, except the imposter, who only sees the category (if that setting is on).
- Take turns giving one word linked to the secret word. Go around 1 to 3 times.
- Vote for who you think the imposter is. Most votes is out; a tie means nobody is.
- A caught imposter gets one guess at the word. Guess right and they still win.
- Points: the imposter gets 2 for escaping or guessing the word; otherwise everyone else gets 1. In online rooms, voting for the real imposter is worth 1 more.
